## 1 · The site's name

It lives in the app under **Configuration → Site**, and it appears top left beside the mark.

## 2 · The mark

The small picture beside it is the project's **project image** — `quartz/static/icon.png`, and
`icon-dark.png` in dark mode. Choose your own in the app under **Configuration → Site → Project
image**: **Choose image…** for light, then **Image for dark mode…** for dark. It replaces the mark
in the header and becomes the site's favicon as well, and a later import of the template leaves
it where it is.

## 3 · This text

Delete the three folders and write your own notes. The design stays.
