## 1 · The site's name

It lives in the app under **Configuration → Site**, and it appears top left beside the mark.

## 2 · The mark

The small picture beside it comes from `quartz/static/qc-mark-light.png` and
`qc-mark-dark.png` — two files, light and dark; the template switches between them as soon
as someone picks dark mode. Put your own beside them **under names of your own** and enter
the two names in the first box under **Plugins → quartz-layout-box**. Don't overwrite the two
files: they belong to the template, and a later import with “Template wins” puts them back.
The same import resets the box as well — then enter the two names again; your files stay
where they are.

## 3 · This text

Delete the three folders and write your own notes. The design stays.
